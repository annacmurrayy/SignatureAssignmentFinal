Support around Community Wellness Hub SLC

These services help you access yoga, meditation, and outdoor spaces with ease. Hours may change seasonally, so check provider websites for the latest details.

Service Categories

  1. Transportation

    • UTA TRAX & Bus (Downtown SLC)
      • Weekdays: 5:30 a.m. – 11:30 p.m.
      • Saturday: 6:00 a.m. – 11:30 p.m.
      • Sunday: Limited service, check route schedules
    • GreenBike SLC Stations
      • Daily: 6:00 a.m. – 11:00 p.m. (seasonal)
      • Ideal for short rides to Liberty Park and City Creek
    • Rideshare Drop-off Zones
      • Available 24/7 near downtown studios and trailheads

  2. Maintenance & Gear Support

    • Local Bike Shops (Downtown & Sugar House)
      • Monday – Friday: 10:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m.
      • Saturday: 10:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.
      • Sunday: Closed or limited hours
    • Outdoor Gear Repair (Ski, Climb, Hike)
      • Tuesday – Friday: 11:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.
      • Saturday: 10:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m.

  3. Cleaning & Facility Support

    • Studio Cleaning & Mat Sanitation
      • Daily: Before 6:00 a.m. and after 9:00 p.m.
      • Deep clean: Weekly, typically Sunday afternoons
    • Trailhead Restrooms (Liberty Park, City Creek, Bonneville Shoreline)
      • Spring – Fall: 6:00 a.m. – 10:00 p.m.
      • Winter: Limited or portable restrooms only

  4. Community Support & Wellness

    • Community Centers & Rec Programs
      • Weekdays: 7:00 a.m. – 9:00 p.m.
      • Saturday: 8:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.
    • Local Counseling & Mindfulness Groups
      • By appointment; many offer evening sessions

  5. Nearby Dining & Refueling

    • Healthy Cafés near Liberty Park & Downtown
      • Weekdays: 7:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m.
      • Saturday – Sunday: 8:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m.
    • Jackson Hole & Park City Day-Trip Dining
      • Many options open daily for breakfast, lunch, and dinner
      • Great for post-hike or ski refueling on weekend trips
